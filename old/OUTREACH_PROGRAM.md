## 6. Campaign Workflow (4-Week Sprint)

### Week 1 – Build & Start
- **Day 1:** Create list, add all 100 prospects to tracker, send WhatsApp 1 to first 30
- **Day 2:** Send any replies received + WhatsApp 1 to next 25
- **Day 3:** Continue batch sending + follow-up 1 to Day 1 prospects
- **Day 4:** Continue batch + follow-up 1 + follow-up 2 to earlier prospects
- **Day 5:** Wrap week 1, review analytics, adjust messaging

### Week 2 – Mid-Campaign Review
- Batch send + follow-ups 1–3 to all un-replied prospects
- Prioritize replies: call back any "Replied" prospects that came in this week
- Identify 5–10 high-priority prospects for personal follow-up

### Week 3 – Conversion Push
- All follow-ups sent (Day 10 + Day 17 complete)
- Schedule calls for "Meeting Booked" prospects
- Begin follow-up cadence on any new replies

### Week 4 – Close & Report
- Final follow-up (Re-engagement sequence)
- Convert qualified prospects to "Client" stage
- Compile a pipeline report: opportunities, value, risk
